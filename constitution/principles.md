# Constitutional Principles

Vex Hospital follows these durable principles:

- **Root cause before treatment.** Reproduce and distinguish hypotheses before consequential repair.
- **Authority before action.** Permission must be established at the action boundary.
- **Evidence before confidence.** Claims do not become facts through repetition.
- **Bounded intervention.** Prefer the smallest reversible treatment with an explicit blast radius.
- **Capability preservation.** Recovery includes proving that required capabilities still work.
- **Independent assurance.** The treating agent cannot be the sole authority for high-assurance recovery.
- **Fail closed.** Missing mandatory evidence cannot silently become a pass.
- **Privacy by minimization.** Keep sensitive evidence local and expose only what the evaluation actually requires.
- **Transparent uncertainty.** Unknown, probable, observed, and confirmed are different states.
- **Learning after failure.** Material incidents should become durable regression, evaluation, adversarial, resilience, routing, security, or verifier-calibration cases where appropriate.
- **Model neutrality.** Agent-specific playbooks may adapt execution but cannot weaken constitutional requirements.
- **Proportionality.** Verification depth scales with risk; process weight must not become its own failure mode.
