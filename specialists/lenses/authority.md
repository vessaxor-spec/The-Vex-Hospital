# Authority Department

## Examines

- request authority;
- delegated authority;
- observed identity;
- permission scope;
- available tools versus authorized tools;
- side-effect ceiling;
- escalation and approval boundaries.

## Preferred evidence

Explicit operator authorization, system/platform controls, identity and permission state, tool scopes, audit traces, and case-specific approval artifacts.

## Questions

- Who authorized the action?
- What exact action class and environment are covered?
- Does capability exceed authority?
- Was an earlier approval incorrectly reused?
- Is authority still valid after the case scope changed?

## Avoid

Possessing a credential, role, tool, or repository permission is not itself authorization to use it.
